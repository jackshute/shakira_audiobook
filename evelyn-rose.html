Evelyn Rose

A multi-award nominated British romance narrator based in the stunning Pacific North West, bringing love stories to life — historical, fantasy, dark or off the charts spicy.

Recent titles Follow on Instagram Contact

A sucker for romance and HEAs, Evelyn loves nothing more than bringing love stories to life. Whether they are historical, fantasy, dark or off the charts spicy, Evelyn’s sultry voice breathes life into stories and their characters.

She is known for her skills with characters and accents, and is able to perform multiple UK regional accents, as well as Irish, Australian, French, and general American accents.

  • UK Regional
  • Irish
  • Australian
  • French
  • General American

When she’s not fulfilling desires in the booth, you’ll likely find her wrapping her mouth around a delicious firm and full-bodied … glass of red.
